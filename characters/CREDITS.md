# Character GIFs

TaskMon lists every `.gif` in this folder under **right-click → Character**. Drop in your own GIFs to add more. Small pixel-art sprites are scaled by whole numbers so they stay crisp; larger GIFs are smoothly scaled down to fit the taskbar.

The GIFs in this folder were converted from public-domain (CC0) sprites on OpenGameArt. No attribution is required, but credit is given here anyway:

| File | Source | Author | License |
|---|---|---|---|
| `fox.gif` | [Fox animated](https://opengameart.org/content/fox-animated) (run frames) | hdst | CC0 |
| `dog.gif` | [Dog Spritesheets](https://opengameart.org/content/dog-spritesheets) (brown, row 2) | Jason of GDN | CC0 |
| `wolf.gif` | [Pixel wolf](https://opengameart.org/content/pixel-wolf) (run cycle, background removed) | alizard | CC0 |
| `black-cat.gif` | [Cat sprites](https://opengameart.org/content/cat-sprites) (`catrunx2.gif`) | Shepardskin | CC0 |

The built-in cat, parrot and horse runners are compiled into `taskmon.cpp`. They come from [RunCat 365](https://github.com/runcat-dev/RunCat365), Copyright 2025 Takuto Nakamura, under the Apache License 2.0 (see `../THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md`).
